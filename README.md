## The Best Meal You Ever Had

Write one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal you ever had. A judge model reads the sentences two at a time and keeps the one that makes the meal feel most real.

This entry remembers a bowl of soto ayam ladled out by the entrant's mother at 2 a.m. after a flight home, its turmeric broth steaming up their glasses while the whole house slept.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`): one sentence of plain text, at most 200 characters.
